Services & Pricing

Everything you need to record, mix, and release — under one roof.

01 — Recording

Studio Recording Sessions

Our main tracking room is built around a vintage Neve console with a full complement of outboard gear — compressors, preamps, and effects that give your recordings warmth and depth you can't get in-the-box.

Choose from our Basic package for a single room with our house engineer, or step up to Premium for full multi-room access, isolation booths, and a senior engineer with major-label credits.

Basic: $65 / hr  |  Premium: $120 / hr

02 — Production

Mixing & Mastering

Send us your stems and let our engineers handle the rest. We mix in a dedicated control room tuned for accuracy, then master for streaming platforms, digital download, and vinyl.

Turnaround is typically 5–7 business days per song, with one revision included. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

$200 per song — stems required

03 — Vocal Recording

Vocal & Instrument Tracking

Our isolation vocal booth is treated for near-zero room noise and equipped with large-diaphragm condenser microphones, a pop filter, and a headphone mix system so you can perform at your best.

Standalone vocal tracking sessions are available at our standard hourly rate and can be added to any existing booking.

Included with any studio session booking

04 — Podcast & Voice-over

Podcast Production Suite

Our dedicated podcast suite seats up to four hosts with individual mic setups, noise-isolated walls, and a simple interface that lets you focus on the conversation. Editing and export are included in the hourly rate.

Available for recurring bookings with priority scheduling for weekly shows. Remote guest integration is also available upon request.

$45 / hr — setup, recording, editing & export included
